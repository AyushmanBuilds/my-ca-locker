The person behind the product

The Story Behind MY CA LOCKER.

MY CA LOCKER started with a simple observation: CA firms manage critical client information every day, yet too much of that work can still depend on scattered files, repeated follow-ups and disconnected workflows.

AA
Ayushman AcharyaFounder & Developer · MY CA LOCKER

The Problem

Documents can live across email, WhatsApp, drives and physical folders. Sharing, tracking and finding the right file can become unnecessarily time-consuming.

Why I Built It

I wanted to turn that everyday friction into a focused digital workspace where firms can organize clients, documents, filings and communication in one place.

The Approach

Keep the experience practical: the right people get the right access, clients get a simple portal, and teams get a clearer workflow.

The Vision

Build useful technology for professional teams in India—one real workflow at a time, with simplicity and trust at the center.

“Good software should remove friction from important work—not add another layer of it.”— Ayushman Acharya
